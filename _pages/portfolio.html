01 — Expertise

Six things I do well, each with a result behind it.

  1. 01

    Experimentation and A/B testing

    Test design, power and sample-size analysis, guardrail metrics, live monitoring and ship-or-stop decisions, on flows with millions of monthly visitors.

    +0.6% conversion, +0.5% revenue per visitor (relative) · Air France-KLM, one-month A/B tests on a flow of 240k visitors a day

  2. 02

    Causal inference and uplift modelling

    Meta-learners (S-, T-, X-, R-learners), heterogeneous treatment effects and persuadables targeting, so budget goes to customers whose behaviour actually changes.

    +30% relative conversion uplift · Senzai, telecom client; +5–15% monthly for a bank · published at ICML 2023

  3. 03

    Recommender systems and personalisation

    Ranking and offer recommendation on booking, checkout and CRM flows, evaluated offline first and validated against a control group before rollout.

    €1M+ incremental revenue per month · Air France-KLM flight ranker and branded-fare recommender

  4. 04

    MLOps and production ML

    Airflow, MLflow, DVC, Docker, Kubernetes and GitLab CI/CD on AWS, GCP or Azure: pipelines that score millions of customers and ship new models in days.

    3M customers and 50M interactions scored in production · Senzai, ML lead · new models shipped in days instead of weeks

  5. 05

    Bayesian modelling and uncertainty quantification

    Gaussian processes, calibrated prediction intervals and sensitivity analysis for decisions that need a confidence level, not just a point estimate.

    80% prediction intervals for well production · TotalEnergies · paper in Computational Statistics and Data Analysis

  6. 06

    Large-scale data engineering

    Feature engineering and pipeline optimisation on datasets of tens of millions of rows with BigQuery, PySpark, Redshift and PostgreSQL.

    Detection runtime cut from 25 h to 8 h · namR, full-factorial plan replaced by an optimised Latin-hypercube design, 50M-row datasets on GCP
