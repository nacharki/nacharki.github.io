01 — Expertise

Six things I'm good at, each with a result behind it.

  1. 01

    Experimentation and A/B testing

    Designing tests that can actually answer the question: the right metric, enough traffic, sensible guardrails, and a clear call at the end. Mostly on booking and checkout flows with tens of millions of visits a month.

    +0.6% conversion, +0.5% revenue per visitor (relative) · Air France-KLM, one-month A/B tests on hundreds of thousands of visitors a day

  2. 02

    Causal inference and uplift modelling

    Meta-learners, heterogeneous treatment effects, persuadables. The point is to spend the budget on people whose behaviour will change, not on people who would have bought anyway.

    +30% relative conversion uplift · Senzai, telecom client; +5–15% monthly for a bank · published at ICML 2023

  3. 03

    Recommender systems and personalisation

    Ranking and offer recommendation on booking, checkout and CRM flows, evaluated offline first and then against a control group before anyone rolls it out to everyone.

    €1M+ incremental revenue per month · Air France-KLM flight ranker and branded-fare recommender

  4. 04

    MLOps and production ML

    Airflow, MLflow, DVC, Docker, Kubernetes and GitLab CI/CD, on AWS, GCP or Azure. Pipelines that score millions of customers and let a new model ship in days rather than weeks.

    3M customers and 50M interactions scored in production · Senzai, ML lead

  5. 05

    Bayesian modelling and uncertainty quantification

    Gaussian processes, calibrated prediction intervals and sensitivity analysis, for the decisions where a point estimate isn't enough and someone needs to know how sure we are.

    80% prediction intervals for well production · TotalEnergies · journal paper in Computational Statistics and Data Analysis, patent application US 2024/0211803 A1

  6. 06

    Large-scale data engineering

    Feature engineering and pipeline optimisation on tens of millions of rows, with BigQuery, PySpark, Redshift and PostgreSQL.

    Detection runtime cut from 25 h to 8 h · namR, a full-factorial plan replaced by an optimised Latin-hypercube design on 50M-row datasets
