## Overview

Contributing to the 'Offer Display Recommender' project to personalize customer product offerings for one of Europe's largest airline groups, optimizing the digital customer experience across millions of travelers.

## Challenge

Air France-KLM needed to:
- Personalize product offerings based on customer preferences and behavior
- Optimize the flight selection experience on their website
- Design rigorous experiments to measure impact of changes
- Translate complex customer data into actionable business strategies

## Solution

### Experimentation Framework

**1. A/B Testing Infrastructure**
- Designed controlled experiments for customer journey optimization
- Implemented statistical testing frameworks for flight selection UX
- Established guardrail metrics to ensure no degradation in key KPIs

**2. Customer Behavior Analysis**
- Analyzed browsing patterns, search behavior, and conversion funnels
- Segmented customers based on travel preferences and historical bookings
- Identified key touchpoints for personalization opportunities

**3. Recommendation Strategy**
- Developed personalization logic for offer display
- Optimized product ranking based on customer propensity
- Integrated real-time behavior signals into recommendation engine

**4. Business Intelligence**
- Created dashboards for stakeholder reporting
- Translated statistical findings into strategic recommendations
- Collaborated with commercial teams to align on business objectives

### Technology Stack

- **Data Platform**: Google BigQuery
- **Analytics**: Python, SQL, statistical testing libraries
- **Visualization**: Business intelligence tools for stakeholder communication
- **Experimentation**: A/B testing frameworks

## Impact

- Enhanced customer experience through data-driven personalization
- Optimized conversion funnel for flight selection
- Enabled evidence-based decision making for marketing operations
- Scaled experimentation capabilities across commercial teams

## Key Learnings

1. **Rigorous Experimentation**: Proper A/B test design is critical in high-traffic environments to detect true effects

2. **Stakeholder Communication**: Translating statistical results into business language drives adoption

3. **Customer-Centric Metrics**: Balancing business KPIs with customer experience metrics ensures sustainable growth
